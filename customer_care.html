FAQ
Do you provide International delivery?
Yes, we offer international delivery to many countries worldwide. You can check if we deliver to your location during the checkout process.
What is your returns policy?
Our returns policy is designed to make your shopping experience stress-free. We accept returns within 30 days of purchase, provided the item is unworn, in its original condition, and with tags attached. Visit our Returns page for detailed instructions on how to initiate a return or exchange.
How do I return an item?
Returning an item is simple! Just visit our Returns page and follow the easy steps outlined to initiate a return or exchange. Once we receive your return, we'll process it promptly and issue a refund or store credit, depending on your preference.
How do I track my order?
Tracking your order is easy! Once your order is shipped, we'll send you a tracking number via email. Simply click on the tracking link provided to monitor the status of your delivery in real-time.
How can I contact your couriers?
For any inquiries regarding your delivery or to arrange special instructions, you can easily contact our couriers directly through the contact information provided in the shipping confirmation email.
What are your delivery options?
We offer a variety of delivery options to suit your needs. During checkout, you can choose from standard shipping, express delivery, or even same-day delivery in select areas. Rest assured, we're committed to getting your order to you as quickly and efficiently as possible.
